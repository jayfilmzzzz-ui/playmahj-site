Play Mahj Privacy Policy

Version 1.3, effective November 13, 2026

Play Mahj ("Play Mahj", "we", "us") makes the Play Mahj app for finding, hosting and joining in-person mahjong meets. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Play Mahj app.

1. What we collect

Account. When you create an account you sign in with Apple. We receive the user identifier Apple assigns to you, and your email address if you choose to share it (Apple may give us a private relay address instead). You then add the name other people will see, and a phone number that only Play Mahj can see.

Profile. The mahjong variants you play, your skill level, your home area (a neighborhood or city you type in, not a GPS fix) and an optional avatar photo.

Meets and RSVPs. The meets you host, the meets you RSVP to, waitlists, the time you accepted the attendance waiver and which version you accepted, and invite links you create or redeem.

Getting in. Optional parking, entrance, door and room notes, and entrance photos or floor-plan images you choose to upload for a meet. The photo picker shares only the images you select. We do not receive the rest of your photo library.

Following and friends. The people and business pages you follow, the people who follow you, the follow requests you send and receive, and whether you set your account to private. Two people who follow each other are friends. Friends can see which meets you are going to only if you leave the "Friends can see my RSVPs" setting on.

Business pages. If you create a page for a club, teacher, shop or venue: its name, category, description, areas, website and Instagram name. To verify that the page is yours we read a public web page you point us to (the public Instagram profile, through Instagram's official service from Meta or the public profile page itself, or the home page of your website) and look for a short code you put there. We never ask for or receive your Instagram password.

Posts, comments and likes. The posts your page publishes (text and one optional photo), the comments you write and the posts you like. Before a photo is uploaded the app removes the location and camera details stored inside it.

Shop items on a page. If you add a product to your page we store the link you paste, and our server reads that product page when you add it to get its name, the address of its picture and its price. To check that the link points to a public website we may look up the website's name with a public internet name service (Cloudflare).

Sharing. When you send a post or a meet to friends inside the app, we store who you sent it to so they get a notice. When you use the iPhone share sheet instead, a short message and a link go to the app you pick and we do not see where they went. That message does not contain the post or the meet itself.

Approximate location. With your permission the app uses your device's approximate (coarse) location to sort nearby meets. We use it on the device and in the request that fetches nearby meets. We do not store a precise location, and we do not keep a history of where you have been.

Reports and blocks. If you report or block someone, or report a meet, a page, a post, a comment or a shop item, we store the report, who and what it is about and the reason so we can act on it.

Device and diagnostics. Standard server logs (IP address, request time, app version) kept briefly for security and debugging. If you turn on notifications, Apple gives us a push token for your device so we can deliver them. To stop abuse we keep a short count of how often an account repeats certain actions (for example posting or reporting); these counts are cleared within days and when the account is deleted.

We do not collect your contacts, your precise location, photos you have not chosen to upload, or any payment information. Play Mahj has no in-app purchases.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: show meets, pages, posts and shop items, let you RSVP and follow, let hosts see who is coming, deliver the notifications you turned on.
  • To keep people safe: enforce our rules, act on reports, keep records of waiver acceptance.
  • To support you when you write to us.
  • To keep the service working: debugging, security, preventing abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not use your data to train AI models.

3. Who can see what

  • Other players. Your name, avatar, variants, skill level and home area are visible to people using the app. Your phone number and email address are never shown to hosts or other users.
  • Hosts. A host sees the guest list for their meet: names and avatars of confirmed guests.
  • Meet viewers. Getting in notes and attached images are visible to people who can view the meet. For open meets, this includes people browsing without an account. Do not upload private access codes or images you do not want those viewers to see. You can edit the notes and remove an image from the meet.
  • Friends. Which meets you are going to, if you allow it.
  • Following. A person you follow can see you in their list of followers. If their account is private they get a notice that you asked to follow them, and you get a notice when they accept. Your own lists of who you follow and who follows you are shown only to you. If your account is private, people have to ask before they can follow you.
  • Business pages, posts and comments. A verified page, its posts, their like counts and its shop items are visible to people using the app. A comment shows your name to people who can see the post. The owner of a page can delete comments on its posts.
  • People you share with. A friend you send a post or meet to sees your name on the notice. A shared link opens the post or meet in the Play Mahj app. Someone who does not have the app sees only a page on playmahj.site saying that something was shared with them; that page shows nothing from the post or meet.
  • Service providers. We host the service on Supabase (database, authentication and file storage, in the United States). Apple provides sign-in and push notifications. When a business page asks to be verified by Instagram, the page's Instagram name is sent to Meta's Instagram service to read its public profile. These companies process data only to provide their services to us.
  • Shops. The Shop tab and the Shop section of a business page link out to independent retailers (for example mahjong sets and accessories). Product pictures load straight from the retailer's website or the image service it uses, so that service receives your device's internet (IP) address when a picture is shown, the same as when you visit any web page. When you tap a product you leave the app and that retailer's own privacy policy applies. We do not share your account data with them and we do not earn commissions.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect someone's safety, or to enforce our Terms.

4. Keeping your data

Your account and profile stay as long as your account exists. Meets and RSVPs are kept so hosts and guests have a record of past meets. Pages, posts, comments and shop items stay until you delete them or your account; something that was reported may be hidden and kept while it is reviewed, and something we remove for breaking the rules stays on file, hidden, until your account is deleted. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days. Waiver acceptance records, and reports other people made about an account, are kept for as long as we may need them to resolve a dispute or legal claim, which can be longer than the account (a report about a deleted account no longer names it). Reports you made yourself are deleted with your account.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account inside the app (You tab > Delete account) or by emailing us. Deletion removes your profile, avatar, uploaded Getting in images, who you follow and who follows you, RSVPs and the meets you host. It also removes your business page with its posts, post photos and shop items, your comments and likes, the notices in your Activity, and your phone's push token. Removing an image from a meet stops showing it there; the uploaded file may remain in your account's storage until account deletion. We keep the minimum needed for legal records (for example that a waiver was accepted for a past meet, tied to a random identifier rather than your name) and anything we must keep by law.

6. Your choices

  • Approximate location: allow or deny in iOS Settings; the app still works, you just sort meets by hand.
  • Notifications: each type has its own switch in the You tab.
  • Friends seeing your RSVPs: a switch in the You tab.
  • Private account: a switch in the You tab. You can also unfollow anyone, remove a follower, and unfollow a page at any time.
  • Posts, comments and shop items: you can delete your own; a page's owner can delete the page.
  • Blocking and reporting: on every profile and meet.

7. Florida and California residents

Florida residents may ask us what personal information we hold about them, correct it, or delete it. California residents have the right to know what we collect and why, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. To exercise any of these rights, email us from the address on your account. We will verify the request and respond within 45 days.

8. Under 18

Play Mahj is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we delete it.

9. Changes

When we change this policy we will post the new version with a new effective date, and for material changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
