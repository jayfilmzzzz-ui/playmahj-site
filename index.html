Play Mahj

An iPhone app for finding, hosting and joining in-person mahjong meets. American, Riichi, Hong Kong and other styles, at clubs, libraries, cafes and community rooms.

Find a meet

See open tables near you with the style, skill level, time, place and seats left. RSVP in the app. Meets are free to join.

Host a table

Post your own meet at a venue, approve requests, keep a waitlist and tell your guests how to get in.

Follow clubs and shops

Verified pages for clubs, teachers and shops post their meets, news and items from their own stores.

Not on the App Store yet

Play Mahj is being tested with a small group on TestFlight. This page will have the App Store link when the app is released. Questions: playmahjsupport@gmail.com.
