Support

Email playmahjsupport@gmail.com. A person reads every message. Please write from the email address on your Play Mahj account if you have one.

Delete your account

In the app: You tab, then Delete account. This removes your profile, your meets and RSVPs, who you follow, your page with its posts and shop items, and your comments. You can also ask by email.

Report a person, a meet, a page or a post

Use the Safety button on a meet or a profile, the menu on a page, or the flag on a post, comment or shop item. Reports are private. If someone is in danger, call 911 first.

A page or post was hidden

Pages, posts, comments and shop items that several people report are hidden until a person reviews them. Email us if you think that was a mistake.

Privacy requests

To ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it, email us. The Privacy Policy explains what we collect.
