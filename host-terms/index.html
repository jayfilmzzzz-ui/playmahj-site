Version 1.1, effective November 10, 2026

These Host Terms apply when you list a meet on Play Mahj. They add to the Terms of Service, which still apply to you as a player. By publishing a meet you agree to both.

1. You run the meet

You are the host. Play Mahj is a listing service; we are not a party to the meet, we do not attend, and we are not responsible for what happens there. You decide who is approved, how many seats there are, and what the house rules are, within these terms.

2. Honest listings

Your listing must be accurate: the real date and time, the real address or venue (shared with approved guests only, if you choose), the variant and skill level, how many seats, and any cost that guests should expect from the venue (for example a cafe minimum). Do not list meets you do not intend to hold. Cancel in the app as soon as you know a meet is off, so guests are told.

3. The venue

You are responsible for having the right to use the venue, whether it is your home, a friend's home, a club, a cafe or a rented room. If a venue has its own rules, fees, age limits or capacity, you must follow them and tell guests what applies. Play Mahj does not inspect venues, provide insurance, or pay venue fees. If you rent a venue, that contract is between you and the venue.

4. No money play

You may not run a game for money or anything of value at a listed meet, charge entry for a prize pool, or let guests do so, regardless of the limited penny-ante exceptions in Florida Statutes section 849.085. You may ask guests to cover a venue cost at face value (for example splitting a room fee) if the listing says so in advance. Breaking this rule ends your hosting privileges.

5. Safety

Keep the meet safe and welcoming: no alcohol for anyone under 21, no weapons, no harassment, no discrimination. Remove anyone who makes others unsafe. If someone is hurt, get help first; report serious incidents to us afterward. Guests are adults who attend at their own risk, but you must not create hazards and you must take reasonable care in the space you control.

6. Guest information

You will see guests' names and avatars so you can run the meet. Play Mahj does not give you guests' phone numbers. Use that information only for the meet: confirmations, directions, last-minute changes. Do not add guests to lists, share their numbers, or contact them for other purposes without their permission. Delete the information when you no longer need it.

Because you control the meet and the venue, you are responsible for them. You will defend and indemnify Play Mahj, its owners, staff and contractors against any claim, loss or expense (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of a meet you host, your venue, your guests' injuries or property damage, your breach of these terms, or any law you break while hosting, except to the extent caused by Play Mahj's own gross negligence or willful misconduct.

8. Removal

We may unpublish a listing or end your hosting privileges if a listing is inaccurate, money play is suspected, guests report unsafe or unwelcoming behavior, or you repeatedly cancel late. We will tell you why when we can.
